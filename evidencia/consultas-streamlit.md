## What instrumentation does ISO 1996-2 require for environmental noise measurements?

_2026-09-22 01:11_ · afinado: zero-shot · RAG: rag-anclado, top_k 5

**Modelo afinado**

The instruments for measuring sound pressure levels, including microphones, cables, windscreens, recording devices and other accessories if used, shall meet the requirements for class 1 instruments of IEC 61672-1 for a random-incidence application, and the filters shall meet the corresponding class requirements. The filters may be of the Bessel, Linkwitz-Riley or Octave type. The overall uncertainty of the measurement system, including all calibration uncertainties, shall be better than +/- 3,5 dB for measurements inside buildings and better than +/- 2,5 dB for measurements outdoors.

**Referencia**

The instrumentation system, including microphone, windscreen, cable and any recorders, shall meet the requirements of either a class 1 or a class 2 instrument as specified in IEC 61672-1:2002; competent authorities may require class 1. A windscreen shall always be used for outdoor measurements. For octave-band or one-third-octave-band measurements, class 1 and class 2 systems shall meet the requirements for class 1 or class 2 filters respectively in IEC 61260:1995. Class 1 instruments are specified for air temperatures from -10 C to +50 C and class 2 instruments from 0 C to +40 C.
